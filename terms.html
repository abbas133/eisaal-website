7. Prayer times, qibla and calendar

Prayer times are calculated on your device using the Ja'fari (Shia Ithna-Ashari) method, from your approximate location or the city you set, and are presented as three prayer windows: Fajr, Zohrain and Maghribain. The qibla direction is derived from your device’s compass and your location. Hijri dates depend on the calculation method used and on local moon-sighting decisions, which legitimately differ between communities.

All of these are calculated approximations. Sensor accuracy, magnetic interference, elevation, high-latitude conditions, an out-of-date location and differences between calculation conventions all affect the result. Where precision matters for the validity of an act of worship, verify against your local mosque, your marja’s office, or a physical compass.
