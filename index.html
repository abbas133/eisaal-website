A companion for every day of the Islamic year

Prayer times by the Ja'fari method, the complete Quran, duas, ziyarat and aamaal, organised around the Hijri calendar. Free, with no ads and no account needed.

For iPhone and iPad on iOS 15.5 or later, and Android phones and tablets on Android 10 or later.

Inside the app

  • Today

    The prayer window that is open now, when it closes, and the one after it.

  • What the day asks of you

    The aamaal for today's weekday, month and occasion, with a daily verse and hadees.

  • The complete Quran

    Translation you can switch on or off, verse search, and your reading place remembered.

  • Duas by purpose and occasion

    Kumayl, Tawassul, Nudba and the rest, alongside Munajat, Salawat and the Sahifas.

  • Arabic set with care

    Choose the typeface and size, with coloured harakat and the translation beneath.

  • Ziyarat by personality

    Find a ziyarat by the Masoomeen (a.s.), by occasion or by place.

  • A timetable for any city

    A month of Fajr, Zohrain and Maghribain, with reminders at the exact computed time.

  • Tasbeeh

    A counter with presets for the tasbeeh you recite, and your recent sessions.

  • Khums, worked out

    Enter your savings and assets to see the Sahme Imam and Sahme Sadat shares.

  • Qaza, qibla and the calendar

    Track missed prayers and fasts, find the qibla, and convert Hijri dates.

Made for the Shia Ithna-Ashari community

The Ja'fari method only

Three prayer windows, Fajr, Zohrain and Maghribain, with per-prayer minute adjustments. Times and qibla are calculated on your device.

Texts reproduced verbatim

Every Arabic text, translation and transliteration comes from a published source, unedited.

Works offline

The Quran and every supplication are bundled with the app, so reading needs no connection.

No ads, no account needed

Use everything as a guest. An optional account syncs favourites and the qaza tracker across your devices.
