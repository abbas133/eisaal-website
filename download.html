Download

Get Eisaal

Free on iPhone, iPad and Android. Choose your store below — or open this page on your phone and it will take you there.

iPhone and iPad need iOS 15.5 or later; Android phones and tablets need Android 10 or later.

On a computer?

Scan this code with your phone's camera. It opens eisaal.com/download, which takes an iPhone or iPad to the App Store and an Android phone to Google Play.

Already have the app?

This link works for updates too: if Eisaal is installed, the store page shows Update when a newer version is available, or Open when you are up to date.

Android without Google Play? Some phones, including recent Huawei models, do not include the Play Store. Eisaal is only published on Google Play and the App Store, so it cannot be installed on those devices at the moment.
